[Governed improvement workflows and paired model–harness attribution](improvement_workflows.md) describe the default-off PR 9 integration.

[Unified research studies and reproducibility](research_ablation_suite.md) prepares default-off study overlays and private independently authenticated reports.

[Inspectable evidence workspace](evidence_workspace.md) adds an optional source-linked index with existing access checks; native builder RL and latent memory remain unsupported.

[TF-OPD numerical research](specialist_fusion.md) adds opt-in CPU input moments and immutable fusion proposals; full model fusion and research effectiveness remain unsupported.

Optional [intrinsic-dynamics diagnostics](intrinsic_dynamics.md) measure frozen action
accessibility and supplied rollout-cost consistency separately; no learned-model effectiveness
or causal-recovery claim follows from these probes.

Optional [knowledge-transfer text proxies](knowledge_transfer.md) score saved public source,
target and application answers with declared exposure controls and complete denominators.
Native multimodal training and empirical transfer remain Unsupported.

Optional [advisory specialist participation](specialist_participation.md) independently
reviews candidates before requesting bids. Exact positive maxima can produce recommendations;
no action, reward or promotion is authorized. Native MPT training and empirical quality remain
Unsupported. The interface is Experimental and disabled by default.

Optional [family-search preparation](family_search.md) binds structural hypothesis families,
matched task/seed/budget rosters and scheduled donor transfers to existing candidate protocols.
Journal diagnostics retain failed attempts and unknown costs. Independent outcome auditing and
governed promotion retain authority; live search and comparative effectiveness remain Unsupported.

[Latent-memory source qualification](latent_memory_research.md) records checked MEMORILLA artifacts,
external-runtime requirements and the existing Study F evidence boundary; native execution is Unsupported.
