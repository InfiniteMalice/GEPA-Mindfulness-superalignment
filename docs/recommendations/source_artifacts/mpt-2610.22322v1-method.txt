The paper selects the highest participation bid, samples exact ties uniformly,
and skips all-zero turns. Bids also propagate downstream value backward for
action learning. Separate counterfactual branches from a restored contested
state produce unequal-return bidding preferences. Action and bidding updates
alternate; this note does not reproduce training or transfer reported gains.
