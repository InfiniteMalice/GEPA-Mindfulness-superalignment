A builder creates reusable hierarchical synthetic files with references to raw documents.
CreateFile/MergeFiles/Delete/Stop operate on synthetic structure; raw documents remain available.
The answerer is frozen. GRPO trains the builder from hidden training-query answerability probes
and terminal correctness. The main run uses no explicit navigation/construction-cost shaping.
Evaluation questions are unavailable during construction and checkpoint selection.
GEPA adapts source-linked indexing and authorized readout only; no builder training is reproduced.
