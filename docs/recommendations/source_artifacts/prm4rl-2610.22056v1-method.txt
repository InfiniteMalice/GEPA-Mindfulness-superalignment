Mohan Zhang, Yue Deng. NeurIPS 2025; DOI 10.52202/085713-3370.
Progress reward: R_PRM = gamma * Phi(s_next) - Phi(s) + I_success * r_bonus.
Shaping component: F = gamma * Phi(s_next) - Phi(s).
The full method generates subtask progress functions and augments robotic state
with subtask priors. Mindfulness implements only discrete verified milestones
and the shaping component, with zero terminated potential and unscored truncations.
