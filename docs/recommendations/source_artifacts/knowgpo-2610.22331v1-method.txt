Atomic-condition state: s_t = s_(t-1) OR delta_s_t.
Incremental gain: r_t = ||s_t||_1 - ||s_(t-1)||_1.
Algorithm 1 groups non-answer steps by s_(t-1), despite equation (5)'s index.
Step advantage: (r_t - group_mean) / (group_std + epsilon).
The full method mixes episode and step advantages and uses a clipped policy objective.
