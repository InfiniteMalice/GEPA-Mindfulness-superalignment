The solver and proposer use the same frozen model and harness in fresh sessions.
The proposer receives complete task records, harness code, notes and previous update records.
Engineering checks constrain edits to harness scope, require a code change besides notes,
enforce added-line budgets and a mock-model run. A failed check leads to repair.
Multi-task evolution uses disjoint fresh training batches and separately held-out evaluations;
continued evolution adds a new domain with old success anchors. Notes retain update experience.
Added-line caps follow a declared cosine schedule; this is not an optimizer update equation.
GEPA does not adopt engineering checks as admission or grant unrestricted editing freedom.
Existing protected receipts and external authority remain required. Procedure proposals and
matched 2x2 model/harness attribution are original research adaptations, not a reproduction.
