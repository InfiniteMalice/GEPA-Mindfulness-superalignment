# MDAC source review

Reviewed 24 November 2026. All 17 identifiers resolve to the papers linked below. Each entry distinguishes the paper's result from its possible use in MDAC. The published benchmark outcomes belong to the original experiments. The papers do not evaluate this repository or establish that its mechanism labels recover latent model causes.

| Paper and evidence | Method and assumptions in the paper | Proposed MDAC use and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AeroRSI, Wu et al., Appendix algorithm S1. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24077) | The current navigation policy continues from each proposed repair at a restored failure state. The reproducibility check tests the original action, and a repair must qualify on two independent continuations. Repeated rounds expose failures downstream of earlier corrections. | **Adapt for repair and curriculum.** Record the anchor, parent policy, reproduction, horizon, progress criterion, confirmatory runs and retention examples. A planner's suggestion or an immediate local improvement cannot qualify a repair by itself. Limited-horizon simulator verification depends on restorable state. |
| Safe Personal RSI, Pei et al., v2, Investigation and Repair, Adoption, and Experimental Design. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.23651) | With fixed weights and runtime, test parent and edited instructions, replay reported failures, temporarily revert changed instructions to localize problems, bound repairs and retain the parent when admission fails. The gate uses empirical risk and utility rules with a severe-case veto. Evaluation involved 29 synthetic-service cases and a draft-only assistant. | **Adapt for intervention and admission.** Version candidate and evaluator, preserve authorized positive controls, keep repair bounded and separate safety and utility. The paper's thresholds and case coverage do not establish population safety or authority to promote. |
| GUARD, Zhang et al., §§4.1–4.5, Eqs. 3–4 and Algorithm 1. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.23476) | Grade a fixed pre-execution checklist using traces and executable predicates. Across matched tasks, hurt is the positive part of prior minus current criterion obedience. Gap is one minus current obedience. Candidate pooling and a Soft-HURT gate use a finite buffer and a rejection ledger. | **Direct precedent for criterion transitions.** Keep all four binary transitions and report missing denominators. Paired regressions may guide repair while severe MDAC criteria remain separately vetoed. Paper guarantees concern the gated buffer, with further uncertainty from semantic grading. |
| Agentic Harness Engineering Beta, Lin et al., evolution protocols and human A/B evaluation. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24924) | Triage verifies reported issues, an architect consolidates related requirements, a product role mines recurring or high-impact implicit requirements, and developers produce reviewed changes. Benchmarks are held for evaluation. Human users preferred the system in 31 of 48 valid pairs. | **Adapt for grievances.** Preserve the reported need, provenance, reason for consolidation, review and disposition. Simulated reports are hypotheses. The experiment supplies no mandate for representing affected people or resolving value conflicts. |
| Asking but Missing, Hu et al., §4.4 and human coverage audit. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.23864) | Grounded Code Generation asks which computational choices require clarification, supplies evidence to resolve them, then executes the program. Earliest-loss attribution distinguishes missed decisions from failed grounding or application. Precision and recall depend on a reference interpretation and semantic matching. In-depth coverage was measured on failures from four representative models. | **Direct precedent for phase-linked clarification diagnostics.** Track relevant decisions asked, unnecessary questions, evidence grounding, and final use. Freeze reference decisions or retain legitimate alternatives. Precision and recall from selected failures cannot estimate rates on all tasks. |
| Counterfactual World Models, Balestriero et al., Eq. 1 and identification analysis. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24778) | Approximate an alternative outcome with the observed outcome plus the difference of alternative and factual predictions under the same disturbance. Unpaired factual transitions alone do not identify individual counterfactuals. The small-time result assumes suitable smooth dynamics with separate action and disturbance channels and compatible representations. Discontinuities, folds and command-dependent disturbances break the approximation. | **Conditional adaptation for counterfactual PEO.** Preserve the disturbance, units, factual anchor, evidence type and assumptions. Use matched intervention replays as validation. Permission changes and discrete tool actions provide failure controls for residual reuse. |
| Worth Another Look, Kang et al., Algorithm 1 and limitations. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24187) | Receding-horizon beam search predicts inspection gains while an exact action mask enforces known tariffs, legality and episode length. A free stop is always legal. Dynamic pricing and fallback mitigate premature stopping. The eight-step frozen-detector setup uses explicit per-query prices. | **Adapt for bounded diagnostic allocation.** Keep costs, legal actions and remaining budget exact while comparing predicted and realized diagnostic gain. The paper evaluates detection quality and cannot validate an MDAC severity scale, allocation formula or budget reserve. |
| Thinking Fast, Anywhere, Li et al., §3.3 and Eq. 4. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.23751) | AnyS1 compiles enumerable procedural choices and trains a pointer model on grounded workflow states. It defers below a threshold estimated on separate calibration decisions. A warm threshold targets at least 95% agreement with the slow model. Costs and task success are compared with compiled and uncompiled slow-model workflows. | **Adapt for selective routing.** Restrict fast decisions to enumerated choices and measure coverage, error, deferral, cost and held-out reliability. Teacher agreement can include teacher errors. Confidence cannot establish action authorization. |
| F³, Huang et al., §§3.2–3.4 and Eq. 3. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24708) | Extract locally verified progress from either successful or failed rollouts. Package a precondition, an operation and its effect with the trajectory interval. Fit learns whether an item helps here, and Fusion injects an input prefix and action prior. Minimality holds over candidate clips proposed. | **Adapt for training record selection.** Require evidence of the effect and the applicable state, preserving native behavior when no record fits. Compare held-out use with and without the record. A verified clip can coexist with failed continuation, permissions or eligibility. |
| KnowledgeClaw, Luo et al., §4.2, Eqs. 5–7. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24294) | Typed entities and hyperedges retain source-file provenance. Document deletion prunes support and dependent index entries. Refresh composes deletion and addition. The invariant establishes live source support after a commit. Benchmark questions can query only the committed store. | **Adapt for memory lifecycle.** Test deletion of the only supporter, surviving independent support and stale indexes after revision. Provenance soundness says that evidence is still present. Semantic truth, independent authority and derivation correctness each require separate checks. |
| Resolution as Attention, Xiang et al., memory design and limitations. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24422) | Render previous steps as images and assign resolution from a teacher-trained relevance estimator, then optimize the tradeoff against next-decision consistency. Savings depend on a vision-capable backbone that can read the rendering. Different layouts and OCR behavior can shift performance. | **Exploratory analogy for memory compression.** Evaluate multiple detail levels against full-history decisions, budgets and restoration of a relevant fact. A text summary or pointer is an adaptation. The reported visual-token savings cannot be assigned to it. |
| Self-similar Organization of Information in Language, Lufkin et al., §§2–4. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.26935) | Evaluate changes in model code length as different context bands become available, using matched byte positions in long documents. A scaling collapse supports self-similarity over measured corpus scales. Ordinary model code-length gain is a proxy for mutual information and need not bound it. | **Exploratory memory ablation.** Compare near, intermediate and distant evidence in held-out tasks, conditioning on nearer context where appropriate. The paper describes corpus-level predictability. It supplies neither a per-trace compression policy nor a law for MDAC memory. |
| WorldRuler, Zhai et al., v2, §§3.2–3.4. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24869) | An executable rule graph, object bindings and progress persist across generated video windows. Perception receives independent frames without desired outcomes as answer cues. Prescribed state and observed realization can disagree. Accepted feedback updates later windows. | **Adapt for prescribed and observed state.** Timestamp expectation, observed evidence and discrepancy separately. Check their persistence through checkpoint restoration. A symbolic prescription alone cannot verify a completed external action. |
| From Points to Trajectories, Zhu et al., v2, CounterPoint and supplementary theory. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24249) | Under synchronized sensing and stationary recording conditions, time pooling can erase event-specific fluctuations. CounterPoint subtracts each modality's temporal mean and compares aligned frame-level fluctuations. Twin physical events under identical conditions test the distinction. | **Exploratory temporal instrumentation.** Align semantic events, center features, compare aligned and shuffled traces, and report missing matches. Similarity depends on alignment and does not identify a causal circuit or hidden intent. |
| Memorization and Generalization in Generative Models, Pham, chapters on image and discrete text diffusion. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.26797) | Compares attraction basins and distance to training and synthetic neighbors over a transition in data scale. Discrete diffusion experiments corrupt and reconstruct held-out sentences and analyze conditional entropy. Retrieval of training examples and generalization can coexist. | **Exploratory diagnostics of transfer.** Compare exact repeats, surface variants and held-out families, with leakage controls and novelty measures. A short trace or nearest-neighbor match cannot identify a learned energy landscape or prove memorization. |
| A Foundation Model to Measure and Predict Human Behaviour, Ye et al., Chiron objectives and §5. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24830) | Chiron jointly trains construct estimation, masked individual-response prediction and population-distribution alignment. Evaluations distinguish individual outcomes from group variance and effect magnitude. Collapsed variance can inflate standardized effects and apparent significance. | **Exploratory evaluator calibration.** Compare ratings with independent human evidence and inspect differences across groups, distributional spread and uncertainty. Simulated respondents have no standing to speak for a stakeholder or adjudicate a grievance. |
| QDA, Li et al., §§4.1–4.2 and §5. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.23388) | Search over quantization recipes uses a served-checkpoint divergence proxy on fixed non-test traces, logs and targeted checks, then measures task accuracy. KL ranked 82% of 44 baseline/recipe pairs consistently with accuracy. A lower-KL candidate still truncated long outputs. | **Adapt for cheap screening and independent evaluation.** Compare proxy rankings with held-out task outcomes, record cost and detect tail failures. Transfer of a KL cutoff to alignment, safety or admission requires its own evidence. |

The 16-mechanism taxonomy, channel allocation rules, governance authority, directional-regret severity, disturbance choice for language tasks, and alignment-first training advantage are project hypotheses or design decisions. A controlled fixture can test contract behavior or recovery of its injected fault. A model-backed comparison needs independently scored, unseen task families and comparable data, updates and evaluation costs.
